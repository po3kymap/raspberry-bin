Raspberry Bin

Team ID: 27


What we want to build?

We want to improve the current sorting approach, and automate it, instead of forcing people to think every time about the correct way of throwing wastes into the bin. The system that we want to build is supposed to automatically identify and sort wastes. The user places one item into the chute. The lid locks and drops the item to the scanning area and the camera takes a picture to recognize the item. The system identifies the category of the time and using servomotors, directs the item to the correct bin. We want to support all categories of general waste, pmd, organic, and paper.

What we have done so far?

We looked into different mechanisms such as having a stepper motor and having 4 bins in 0, 90, 180, and 270 degrees and dropping them using a chute system. However, due to the lack of a stepper motor or a 360 degree servo in STORES Utwente and taking a long time for them to arrive we have replaced the design with a seesaw design using 3 different seesaws. 

We made 4 different 3D models of our designs and concepts in onshape.com which resulted in our confidence in what we are building due to having a certain goal/product outcome in our mind and working towards that. 

Wrote down our exact plan with all the sizes that we need to cut including manual scrap wood cutting and also laser cutting and also the list of all the materials that we need to buy.
